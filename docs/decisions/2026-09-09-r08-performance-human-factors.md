# R08.3 uses descriptive local metrics and explicit human-factors data

The Companion needs evidence about interaction cost and understanding without presenting synthetic timings or model self-evaluation as proof of a user benefit. The performance command therefore measures one deterministic local projection workload, records its environment and percentiles, and explicitly lists every product or system lane that it cannot measure. It never applies a hidden pass threshold.

The human-factors report accepts only anonymized outcome rows bound to a candidate SHA. It rejects raw selection text, URLs, prompts, answers, credentials, email fields, empty input, duplicate observation identifiers, and candidate mismatches. It emits a descriptive condition summary and a clear limitation rather than an automatic “improved” conclusion. Real participant work requires consent, a frozen protocol, a baseline/Companion comparison, and separate review of task correctness and workload.

The product measures perception enhancement as reduced task switching, material confusion, and recovery cost. Locale selection controls interface copy; translation remains outside the product objective and acceptance scope.
